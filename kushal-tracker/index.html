Add Entry

Investment = jo paisa aapne kisi se liya business me lagane ke liye. Levana = jo paisa aapko doosron se lena hai. Devana = jo paisa aapko doosron ko dena hai. "Badhao" se balance badhta hai (naya udhaar/purchase), "Ghatao" se ghatta hai (payment kiya) — total hamesha current/live balance dikhata hai. Upad ab Expenses & Cash tab me hai.
Teeno Cash Balance se automatically linked hain: Levana Badhao (udhaar diya) = Cash kam, Ghatao (wapas mila) = Cash zyada. Devana Badhao (naya udhaar liya, abhi payment nahi) = Cash par asar nahi, Ghatao (payment kiya) = Cash kam. Investment Badhao (paisa mila) = Cash zyada, Ghatao (wapas kiya) = Cash kam. Entry delete karne se uska ulta apne aap ho jaata hai.
Person-wise Balance table neeche Other Sale tab ki pending entries bhi combined dikhata hai (usi person ke Devana/Levana ke saath jud jaati hai) — entries khud "🎁 Other Sale" tab me dalni hain.

Opening Balance

Ye ek fixed baseline number hai — is Month ke Net Worth (Bank+Portal+Stock+Cash+Levana−Investment+Upad) ka total, jo purani (Excel wali) accounting se liya gaya hai. Agle month ka Monthly Profit calculate karne ke liye is baseline se shuru hota hai. Ek baar set karne ke baad usse pichle mahino ka data granular daalne ki zarurat nahi.

Backup & Restore

Data aapke apne Supabase cloud me save hota hai — file ek baar bhejne ke baad dobara bhejne ki zarurat nahi, sab PCs par hamesha latest data khud aa jata hai. Phir bhi time-time par Export karke JSON backup safe rakhna acha rehta hai (Export dabane par data ek box me khulega, wahan se copy karke Notepad me paste-save kar lena).

🗑️ Delete History galti se delete hui entries yaha milengi

Jab bhi koi entry delete hoti hai (Sale, Expense, Stock, Bank, Udhaar, Other Sale, Panel), uski copy yaha save ho jaati hai. Restore dabate hi entry wapas apni jagah jud jayegi. Ye history bhi cloud me sync hoti hai, isliye sab PCs par dikhegi (last 200 deletes tak).
